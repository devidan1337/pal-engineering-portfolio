# 10 — PAL-VOICE-001 Voice Interface Design

Disposition: **Merged** as a roadmap initiative.

Purpose: Explore a voice-first interface. Design sketch: push-to-talk with private access, permission-aware retrieval and human approval for consequential actions; speech processing and an approval gate are conceptual, not deployed components.

The long-term initiative is approved and a prototype is planned; implementation and testing are not evidenced. Lab number and hardware selection remain open. Initiative approval does not authorize purchases or deployment. Device-specific details and operational identifiers are omitted.
